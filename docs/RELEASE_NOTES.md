## RecklessMuse 1.1.0

New: **Favorites**

- **♥ LIKE** button in the Programmer section adds / removes the current preset from your favourites.
- **Favorites bank**: first stop of the BANK button; inside it, `<` `>` only browse your liked presets.
- **Click the display** to open a browser menu: Favorites first, then every category (liked presets are marked ♥).
- Favourites are stored in `~/Music/RecklessMuse/Favorites.xml`, shared by every project and plug-in instance, and work for factory and user presets.

Also includes the 1.0.1 fixes (arpeggiator / sequencer stop with the MIDI notes, centred mono sounds).

Download `RecklessMuse-macOS.zip`, unzip, then in Terminal: `cd ~/Downloads/RecklessMuse && ./install.sh`
